# Production ideas: resources, benches, and what they make

Started 2026-09-13. **A collection, not a design.** Three lists to keep adding to; cutting comes
later. The twenty-five recipes in the game today are placeholders and did not constrain this.

Working assumptions so far, all changeable:

* **No wood.** The island is treeless, so no timber, no charcoal, no wooden handles or frames.
* **Buildings are cast or laid.** Limestone burned in a kiln gives quicklime, quicklime with water
  and sand gives mortar and concrete. Walls are stone, brick and concrete.
* **Animals exist**, so hides, bone, horn, fat and sinew are allowed. Rows depending on them are
  marked, because the animals question is still open.
* **No quantities or work times.** That is balance, and balance comes after the shape.

## 2. Benches, and what each one makes

**Kiln.** Quicklime, cement, brick, roof tile, ceramic pots, ceramic insulators, crucibles, filter
housings, glass, coke.

**Mixing trough.** Slaked lime, mortar, concrete, plaster, whitewash.

**Smelter.** Iron bloom, steel, copper ingot, tin ingot, lead ingot, brass, bronze.

**Anvil and forge.** Iron bar, steel bar, nails, hinges, chain, tongs, hammers, pickaxes, shovels,
hoes, sickles, knives, hatchets, spear heads, swords, cooking pots, iron frames, iron ties, shot.

**Rolling press.** Iron plate, sheet metal, wire rod, copper strip.

**Machine shop.** Copper wire, cable, valves, springs, locks, lockpicks, pneumatic rifle, pneumatic
pistol, musket lock parts, gauges, machined parts, steam generator, wind turbine, battery, lamp.

**Tannery.** Tanned leather, rawhide, straps, belts, leather seals, leather cap, jacket, trousers,
boots, gloves, satchel, waterskin.

**Spinning wheel.** Thread, yarn, twine.

**Loom.** Cloth, sacking, canvas, sailcloth, blanket, padded coat, shirt, trousers, dust mask.

**Rope walk.** Cord, rope, netting, bowstring.

**Mill.** Flour, bone meal, crushed ore, ground pigment.

**Oven.** Bread, biscuit, hardtack.

**Kitchen or campfire.** Rations, cooked meals, stew, boiled water, rendered tallow.

**Curing rack or smokehouse.** Salt fish, salt meat, jerky, smoked fish.

**Brewing vat.** Ale, small beer, vinegar.

**Still.** Alcohol, spirits, lamp fuel.

**Oil press.** Cooking oil, lamp oil, seed cake as a byproduct.

**Chemical bench.** Acid, lye, soap, candles, fertiliser, powder *(only if powder is makeable here
at all)*.

**Medical bench.** Bandage kit, splint kit, blood bag, painkiller, stimulant, antiseptic.

**Workbench, the hand-tool tier.** Bows, arrows, slings, torches, baskets, hafts, grenade assembly,
filter cartridges, simple furniture, tool repairs.

**Glassworks**, if it is not just the kiln. Bottles, window panes, lenses, lamp glass, vessels.

**Breaker or salvage bench.** Scrap from broken items, stripped cable, recovered parts, reclaimed
lead.

**Construction site.** Walls, floors, roofs, doors, gates, palisades, ramparts, foundations.

**Things that are gathering rather than crafting.** Well or pump for water. Salt pans. Field or plot
for grain, fibre, oilseed and medicinal plants. Quarry and mine nodes for stone, limestone, clay,
sand, gravel, ore, coal and sulfur.

## What fell out of the lists

Not decisions.

**The kiln sits under four industries.** Limestone through it gives lime for building, the same lime
does hide liming for the tannery and flux for the smelter, clay gives brick and ceramic insulators
for the grid, and sand plus lime gives glass. So the kiln is probably the first real industrial
building a settlement puts up, and everything past hand tools is gated on having fuel to run it.

**Fuel might be the most strategic resource on the island.** Every kiln, smelter, forge and boiler
burns it, and so does the steam generator. With no wood that means peat or coal, so a bog or a seam
could be worth more than an iron mine, because iron with no fuel is just rock.

**Powder probably cannot be made here.** It needs charcoal, charcoal needs wood, and there is no
wood. That matches the lore, which already treats powder as a trade good. Coal char is the only way
out if we want it made locally.

**Steel may be the real tier line, not iron.** Iron does tools and armour. Steel does pressure
tubes, springs and wire, which is what a pneumatic rifle, a lockpick and a machine shop all need.

**Anything light or moulded has to be salvage.** With no wood and no plastics, a panel, a housing or
a case is ceramic, cast metal, or pulled out of the ruins. That pushes the ruins from scenery into a
supply chain.
